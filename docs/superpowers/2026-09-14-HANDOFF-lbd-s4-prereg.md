## ⚠ The three things a reader is most likely to get backwards

**1. The un-listenable drop list is NOT held constant, however the config reads.** It removes
nobody from `V` and nobody from `P` because it is *measured inert* there; it removes nobody from
`U` because over an uncensused population the guard **refuses** and the filter is switched **off**.
Two zeroes that look identical in a configuration dump and mean opposite things — and the arms
where it switches off are exactly the arms that grow the map. This is the pre-registration's §2.4,
the section `CLAUDE.md` requires because a factor table cannot see a dormant term. `LBA-M4` sizes
it, `LBA-G4` discounts `LBA-M3`'s supply gain when it is large, `LBA-X2` carries the bar, and
`ULC-F4` means every one of those figures **over-drops** relative to how the app actually resolves
clips.

**2. `LBA-M3` over a `V` arm is 1.0 by construction, and that is not a finding.** The added set is
exactly `P − V`, so no member of it is in `V`. It is the `V` rule's own definition made visible —
and it is arguably the most decision-relevant line on the page: *the population rule that keeps
today's artists is the rule that keeps none of the artists this track exists to help.* The
consequence is a bar: **`LBD-G2` is applied within a population rule and never across one**
(`LBA-X6`).

**3. `LBD-X6` still stands.** Its condition was discharged on 2026-09-13 and `R10` **fired**, which
confirmed the bar rather than releasing it. `LBA-D1` — the owner's ruling that every `S4` arm uses
ListenBrainz's own pairing — makes the point moot for these arms and **does not license a sentence
about the cheaper form.**

  assumed.** At zero `known` presses the ramp multiplier is exactly `0.0` and the file's own
  comment records that `cost += 0.0 * fame` is numerically identical to omitting the term —
  measured, and pinned by `MSW-G2`. **It follows that only d0 is measured**, and the
  pre-registration says so rather than working around it.
- **`LBA-G2` may stop a cell only on its stated bar.** The executing session does **not** have the
  authority to reduce the lattice for any other reason — not "obviously dominated", not cost.
  `LBA-A9` is the corner and it is the only cell that can say *"there is no bigger map to have
  inside these rules."*
- **Nothing here re-reads any `LBD-` criterion.** `LBA-M3` uses `LBD-C2b`'s statistic and
  `LBD-G2`'s bar over new arms — a new measurement in an old currency, not a re-read.
