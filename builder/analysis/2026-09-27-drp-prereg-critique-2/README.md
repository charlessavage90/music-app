## Findings

### `DRP-AM5-F1`: the relaxation search as worded records `r` > 0 at every press from 4, so `DRP-G9`(c) and (d) fire on correct routing (`DRP-Q10`, `DRP-Q11`)
- **Claim (§14 `DRP-AM3` item 4):** *"The harness finds `c` by searching the frame's distinct
  percentiles at or above `F_max(k)` with the shipped `find_journey`: the lowest one at which it
  returns an interior-bearing journey."* The definition it implements (`DRP-AM2` item 1) is
  `c` = max(`F_max(k)`, `b`), `r` = `c` − `F_max(k)`.
- **Measured:** no scheduled value from k = 4 to k = 20 equals any node's percentile (0 of 17). The
  lowest distinct percentile at or above `F_max(k)` sits 5.7e-07 to 5.1e-05 above it.
- **Derivation:** where the map can route under `F_max(k)` itself (`b` ≤ `F_max(k)`), the worded
  search returns p* = the next distinct percentile above `F_max(k)`, not `F_max(k)`. So:
  - `r` = p* − `F_max(k)` > 0 at **every** (pair, press ≥ 4). `DRP-C13`'s "share with `r` > 0"
    reads 1.0 at every depth whatever the map does;
  - `DRP-G9`(d) compares `c` = p* with the minimax `b` ≤ `F_max(k)` < p* and fires;
  - `DRP-G9`(c) fires wherever `b` is at or below the distinct percentile just under p*;
  - the journey itself can differ from the definition's, because p* admits the nodes sitting at p*
    and `F_max(k)` excludes them.

  Where this can occur on the proxy: 30 of 40 FAMOUS `b₀` are below 0.895, the lowest band ceiling,
  and all 40 MID are.
- **Also:** `DRP-AM2` item 1's *"`c` is always the percentile of some node"* is false whenever `r` =
  0: then `c` = `F_max(k)`, which is never a node's percentile here (measured).
- **Where:** §14 `DRP-AM3` item 4 (first sub-bullet); `DRP-AM2` item 1, the relaxation bullet.
- **Smallest change:** "test `F_max(k)` first. If `find_journey` returns an interior-bearing journey
  with every node above `F_max(k)` excluded, `c` = `F_max(k)` and `r` = 0. Otherwise `c` = the lowest
  distinct percentile **above** `F_max(k)` at which it does." Qualify the "percentile of some node"
  sentence with "when `r` > 0".
- **Changes:** gates `DRP-G9`(c) and (d), which would otherwise fire on correct routing, and the
  descriptive `DRP-C13`. `DRP-R13` is unaffected in practice, because the spurious `r` is below
  1e-4 and so below `N_noise` (derived). No bar's value changes.

### `DRP-AM5-F2`: §2.6's ceiling row is asserted for the ramp's `k` at press 10 only. The floor and avoidance inputs are unasserted wherever the ceiling excludes anything, and double-listing is routine (`DRP-Q8`, `DRP-Q10`)
- **Claim (§2.6, `DRP-AM2` row):** the floor, ramp and avoidance inputs are unchanged by ceiling
  exclusions, *"**Asserted** by `DRP-G9` and `DRP-G5`, not assumed."*
- **Derivation, gate by gate:**
  - `DRP-G5` runs at presses 1 and 10. At press 1 the ceiling is 1.0 and excludes **0** nodes
    (measured: nodes > 1.0 = 0), so it sees no ceiling exclusion there. Only press 10 tests the
    ceiling, and it tests **only** `n_known` (`pathfinding.py:130`).
  - `DRP-G9`(a) and (e) run where the ceiling set is empty.
  - `DRP-G9`(b)–(d) and `DRP-G10` test admission and feasibility. Feasibility does not depend on
    cost, so a leak into `effective_floor_raw` (`:42-43`, counts `KNOWN` + `DISLIKE`) or into
    `avoidance_map` (`:105-107`, takes `DISLIKE`) passes all of them.

  So a ceiling passed as `DISLIKE` would relax the floor at presses 4–6 and penalise the
  neighbourhood of every excluded node at every press ≥ 4, and no gate would fire. The
  `KNOWN`-leak red control covers the ramp only.
- **Measured, the §2.7-shape hazard:** 155 of 160 victims pressed at presses 0–3 sit above
  `F_max(4)` = 0.985. They are identical in `DRP-S0P3` by `DRP-G9`(e). From press 4 on, a pressed
  artist is therefore routinely **both** a `KNOWN` user exclusion **and** above the ceiling. In A0
  no node is ever excluded twice, because a victim is an interior of the current path and so was not
  already excluded. That is why it is inert there, and the ceiling removes the reason.
  `find_path` handles a doubled node correctly: `hard` is a set, and `n_known` counts `KNOWN`
  entries (`:93`, `:130`). **A harness that dedupes its list by node and keeps the `"ceiling"`
  reason undercounts `k` by up to 4 at press 4.** That moves the ramp and, at presses 4–6, the floor.
  `DRP-G5` sees it at press 10 only.
- **Smallest change:** add to `DRP-G9`, at **every** (pair, press) of both ceiling cells:
  - the exclusion list passed to `find_journey` has exactly `k` `KNOWN` entries and 0 `DISLIKE`,
    and every other entry is `"ceiling"`;
  - the shipped `effective_floor_raw` on the passed list equals it on the user list;
  - the shipped `avoidance_map` on the passed list's `DISLIKE` nodes is empty.

  Red control: at press 10, the ceiling passed as `DISLIKE` must fire it.
- **Changes:** a gate (`DRP-G9`) and the §2.6 row's "asserted" claim. No bar's value changes.

### `DRP-AM5-F5`: `DRP-R13` is under-specified, and both of its thresholds come from a different statistic (`DRP-Q9`)
- **Claim (§7 and §14 `DRP-AM2` item 4):** *"Median band `r` ≥ 0.05, with the same supply level's
  ramp cells at NO MOVEMENT or BELOW BAR → missing supply; median band `r` < `N_noise` in the same
  case → pricing"*. It says the effect sizes are *"reused rather than invented"*.
- **Not pinned:**
  - the aggregation: pooled (pair, depth), or median over pairs of each pair's band median, and over
    which pairs (band-readable or not);
  - "the ramp cells": `DRP-P1` **and** `DRP-P2`, or either. `DRP-P0` is not a ramp-strength level.
  - The two readings diverge whenever the two ramp levels disagree.
- **Currency and kind:**
  - 0.05 is `DRP-C1`'s material bar on a paired difference of **median** interior fame. `r` is a gap
    between the **highest** interior's ceiling and the schedule. Same unit (percentile points),
    different statistic.
  - `N_noise` is by construction the sensitivity of median `D` to press-rule randomness (§5). `r` is
    deterministic given the ladder, so "below the noise floor" has no noise meaning for it.
  - Each threshold is therefore a choice with no calibration for `r`. The design's own rule is to say
    so (the 0.90 and 10-point bars do).
- **Can it fire, or is it certain, by arithmetic?** Neither is decided by headroom. The lower bound
  `r` ≥ max(0, `b₀` − `F_max(k)`) makes a per-pair band median ≥ 0.05 certain on only 2 of 40 FAMOUS
  pairs and 0 of 40 MID (measured). The real `r` uses the ceiling cell's own victims, which cannot be
  computed without routing a ceiling (barred). **Unresolved, not vacuous.**
- **Smallest change:** pin "median over band-readable pairs of the per-pair median of `r` over
  presses 7–10", and "both `DRP-P1` and `DRP-P2` at NO MOVEMENT or BELOW BAR". State that 0.05 and
  `N_noise` are reused as yardsticks for a different statistic, a choice stated as one.
- **Changes:** a read (`DRP-R13`'s branch conditions). The values 0.05 and `N_noise` need not change.

## Observations

- **`DRP-AM5-O1` (derived): `DRP-G9`(c) is a self-consistency check, not an independent one.** It
  re-applies the search's own predicate (`find_journey` at the next-lower distinct percentile). The
  independent sides are (d) and `DRP-G10`. That is sound as designed. It is noted so that (c)
  passing is not read as confirming (d).
- **`DRP-AM5-O2` (derived): `DRP-C11` must read the shipped `fame_lb_pctl`, with nulls at 0.0.**
  graph-descriptives' `victim_key` input is a separate array with NaN at nulls
  (`graph_descriptives.py:227`). A minimax built on that array would propagate NaN through Python's
  `max`, which is order-dependent under NaN, and would disagree with a `find_journey` search that
  admits nulls. A0 has 3 nulls (measured).
- **`DRP-AM5-O3` (measured): "exceeds" must be strict, and it matters for exactly one node at
  presses 0–3.** One node sits at exactly 1.0. With `>` the 1.0 ceiling excludes nothing. With `≥`
  it excludes that node, and `DRP-G9`(e) sees it only if that node lies on a `DRP-P0` journey at
  0–3. Pin the comparison as `>` in the harness text.
- **`DRP-AM5-O4` (derived): `DRP-AM1-O5`'s press-1 red control for `DRP-G4` must stay `P1`/`P2`-only.**
  Extended to `DRP-P3` ("`DRP-G4` as extended"), it is certain not to diverge at press 1 (0 nodes
  above 1.0), and it would stop the design. `DRP-AM4`'s 0.99-at-press-1 is the `DRP-P3` analogue.
- **`DRP-AM5-O5` (derived): the forced-stop detour is not a separate mechanism under the ceiling.** A
  least-cost path of length > 2 never uses the direct edge, so `find_journey`'s interior-bearing
  result is the least-cost path over the graph minus `hard` minus the direct edge in both branches
  (`pathfinding.py:219-231`), up to exact-tie handling. A ceiling can relabel `natural` as `forced`
  and change nothing else. Measured: 1 of 40 FAMOUS pairs and 0 of 40 MID are adjacent.
- **`DRP-AM5-O6` (text): two unmarked residues of superseded wording.**
  - `DRP-AM2`'s exposure (i) still reads *"`F_max(1)` = 0.9857 < 0.99, so from the first press…"*,
    unstruck. Only the ELIMINATES sentence after it is struck. The note *"arithmetic stands from
    press 4 on"* is right (0.985 < 0.99), but the numeral and "first press" are the retired
    schedule's.
  - §2.2's heading still says *"The six cells"*, with no ⚑.

  Neither governs anything.
- **`DRP-AM5-O7` (measured): the `DRP-G1` comparison is feasible on the MID half as well.** This
  probe's own ladder reproduces the committed d0/d5/d10 medians 40/40 on MID. The predecessor
  checked FAMOUS only (`DRP-AM1-F7`).
- **`DRP-AM5-O8` (measured): headroom is large on the proxy.** FAMOUS `b₀` median 0.8212 against an
  `X_A0` median of 0.9965 (`H` median 0.1773). MID `b₀` median 0.2351 against `X_A0` 0.9925. So the
  map with no exclusions routes famous pairs well under every band ceiling on most pairs. Per
  `DRP-AM2` item 2 this annotates the priors and changes no read. Proxy draw, 2 T1-like pairs only.

## Claims that do not resolve

- **`DRP-G9`(e)'s red control on the `DRP-` draw:** unmeasured, because the draw is barred. On the
  proxy it **cannot fail for want of a victim**. 38 of 40 press-1 journeys carry an interior above
  0.99, and on all 38 a 0.99 ceiling with relaxation is **forced** to change the journey (m₁ >
  max(0.99, b₁), derived). That includes both T1-like pairs. `DRP-S1P3`'s press 1 is on another map
  and was not measured.
- **Whether `DRP-R13` lands on "missing supply", "pricing" or "mixed"** is not decided by the
  headroom bound (F5). It needs the ceiling cell's own victim chain.
- **The frequency of the `DRP-AM5-F2` dedup hazard in a real harness** depends on how the harness
  builds its list. That code is unwritten.
